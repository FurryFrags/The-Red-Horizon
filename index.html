The Red Horizon: Act 1

Operational Warfare Prototype — Ground, Air, Naval · HP + Organization

Briefing

Turn: 1

Phase: NATO

Selected Province

Select a province to inspect forces.

Combat

Drag from a NATO province to another province to move or attack. Pathfinding will route through connected provinces.

Mechanics

  • Blitzkrieg: Armor gains +30% damage in plains/desert and can move again if target breaks.
  • Encirclement: Enemy provinces cut off from capitals lose 20 organization every turn.
  • Air/Naval: Adds support fire and organization damage during attacks.
